Hello! I am an applied economist and PhD candidate at the University of California, Davis. My research examines behavioral and market responses to environmental regulation, with a focus on the transportation sector. In my job market paper, I study how a US emissions regulation on shipping altered production patterns in the US container shipping industry. 

Prior to joining UC Davis, I earned a BSE in Chemical Engineering from the University of Michigan (Go Blue!), worked as a process engineer for an electroplating company, and earned an MS in Economics from the University of Wisconsin, Madison.
